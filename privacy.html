Privacy Policy

Salotto Ticinese · Last update: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Salotto Ticinese publishes an agenda of public cultural events in Ticino, Switzerland, on Instagram (@salottoticinese). Contact: salottoticinese@gmail.com.

What data we use

  • Public event information (title, date, place, link, images) that institutions publish on their websites, newsletters and public Instagram business accounts.
  • Newsletters that institutions send to our own email address.

We do not collect personal data from visitors or followers. We do not sell or share data. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

How we use Google and Meta services

Our software uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive) only for our own Salotto Ticinese account: to read newsletters sent to us, to keep our event list, and to archive our posts. It uses the Instagram API only to publish our own posts and to read public posts of public business accounts. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Images and credits

We credit the source of each event. If you represent an institution and want a post changed or removed, write to salottoticinese@gmail.com. We act within 7 days.

Data deletion

We keep no personal data about users. To ask for deletion of any data about you or your institution, send an email to salottoticinese@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We delete it and confirm within 30 days.
